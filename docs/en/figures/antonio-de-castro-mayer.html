Bishop of Campos, Brazil (coadjutor bishop from 1948, diocesan bishop 1949–1981), the 'other bishop of 1988': co-consecrator of the Écône consecrations. His priests formed the Priestly Union of Saint John Mary Vianney (1988), root of the Campos line that later reconciled with Rome (2002).[3] [5] [6]

Campos line — from Antônio de Castro Mayer (Brazil)

coadjutor bishop of Campos from 1948, Bishop of Campos 1949–1981; co-consecrator at Écône, 30 June 1988; † 25 April 1991

Consecrated
  • The 1988 Écône four — Fellay, Tissier de Mallerais, Williamson, de Galarreta — Castro Mayer assisted as co-consecrator at their consecration; the principal consecrator was Lefebvre — see the SSPX line[1] [3]
  • Licínio Rangel — São Fidélis (Brazil), 28 July 1991 — consecrated by Tissier de Mallerais, assisted by de Galarreta and Williamson, to lead the Campos priests' union after Castro Mayer's death; † 2002[4] [5]
Canonical status as recorded here
1988 excommunication declared (the 2009 remission concerned the four living bishops)[3]
