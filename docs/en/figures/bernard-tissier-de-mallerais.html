One of the four 1988 bishops; Lefebvre's biographer; principal consecrator of Licínio Rangel for the Campos priests (São Fidélis, 1991); died at Martigny, Switzerland, after a fall, and is buried in the Écône seminary crypt.[1] [8] [9]

Recorded events

5 dated events in this chronology name this figure or have them as their unambiguous actor. A ? flag marks a date not yet verified against a primary source.

YearDatePlaceEvent
1988 1988-06-30 Écône The Écône consecrations — Lefebvre, with Bishop Antônio de Castro Mayer (emeritus of Campos, Brazil) as co-consecrator, consecrates Bernard Fellay, Bernard Tissier de Mallerais, Richard Williamson and Alfonso de Galarreta without papal mandate.[1]
1988 1988-07-01 Rome Excommunications declared — The Congregation for Bishops declares that Lefebvre, Castro Mayer and the four new bishops incurred latae sententiae excommunication.[1] [4]
1991 1991-07-28 São Fidélis, Brazil Rangel consecrated for the Campos union — Licínio Rangel is consecrated without papal mandate by Bishop Tissier de Mallerais, assisted by de Galarreta and Williamson, to lead the Campos priests after Castro Mayer's death.[6] [7]
2009 2009-01-21 Rome 1988 excommunications remitted — A decree of the Congregation for Bishops (dated 21 January, published 24 January) lifts the excommunications of the four bishops; some sources cite 10 March 2009, the date of Benedict XVI's explanatory letter.[5]
2024 2024-10-08 Martigny, Switzerland / Écône Death of Tissier de Mallerais — Bishop Bernard Tissier de Mallerais, one of the four 1988 bishops and Lefebvre's biographer, dies in hospital at Martigny (Valais, Switzerland) after a fall at Écône reported in SSPX communiqués; his funeral is celebrated at the Écône seminary on 18 October 2024 and he is buried in its crypt.[8] [9] [10]
